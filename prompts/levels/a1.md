# Learner level: A1 (beginner)

Use the 500 most common English words. Use present simple, `be`, `have got`,
`can`, and very short past simple forms. Keep sentences under 8 words and use one
idea per sentence. Avoid phrasal verbs, idioms and subordinate clauses. Repeat key
words instead of using synonyms. Ask simple yes/no or `wh-` questions. If the
learner is stuck, offer two ready-made answer options in English.
